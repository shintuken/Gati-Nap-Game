# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Gamers who want to quickly top up a game account by selecting a game, choosing a character and recharge package, and completing payment successfully.

## Product Purpose

Provide a clear, convenient, and trustworthy recharge flow from game selection through successful payment. Success means users can understand the available choices, complete the transaction with minimal friction, and receive clear confirmation that the payment succeeded.

## Positioning

A focused, guided game-recharge experience that reduces decision friction by organizing the flow around the user's game, character, package, and payment choices.

## Operating Context

Users may arrive ready to recharge a known game or may need to choose among available games and characters. The primary workflow is sequential: select a game, select a character, select a recharge package, review the transaction, complete payment, and see a success result.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The core workflow must support game selection, character selection, package selection, payment, and successful transaction confirmation.
- The experience should optimize for clarity, speed, error prevention, and confidence during payment.
- Existing product details, prices, payment behavior, and factual content must not be invented during future design work.
- Visual direction is open for recommendation; no existing brand, palette, typography, or visual constraint is binding.

## Product Principles

- Make the next step obvious.
- Minimize effort between intent and successful recharge.
- Prevent wrong-game, wrong-character, and wrong-package errors before payment.
- Make price, payment state, and transaction outcome unambiguous.
- Preserve user confidence throughout the flow.
